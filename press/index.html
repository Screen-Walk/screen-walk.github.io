Description in 100 words

Screen Walk is an iPhone app that keeps blocking the apps and websites you choose, using Apple’s Screen Time technology. Walking tops up minutes on a transit-style fare card: by default 250 steps top up 1 minute. When you want your apps, press and hold the card on the reader to start a break of 5 to 60 minutes; blocking returns by itself when it ends. Blocking hours let you choose the days and times it runs. Steps and chosen apps stay on the phone. No account, no ads, no streaks and no nagging. 7 free days, then one payment.
