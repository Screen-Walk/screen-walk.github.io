Walk to unlock apps on iPhone

Yes, there is an iPhone app for that. In Screen Walk, walking tops up minutes on a card, and you start a timed break to open the apps you chose.

What people mean by walk to unlock apps

The phrase describes a small group of iPhone apps. They keep chosen apps closed, and you open them by moving. Screen Walk is one of them, and walking is the only way it tops up minutes. Some apps in this group also count push-ups or other workouts; Screen Walk counts steps only.

How Screen Walk does it

  1. Screen Walk keeps blocking the apps and websites you choose. It uses Apple’s Screen Time technology, so the apps themselves stay closed.
  2. Walking tops up minutes. By default 250 steps top up 1 minute on your fare card. Steps come from Apple Health.
  3. You start a break. You choose 5 to 60 minutes and press and hold your card on the reader. Your apps open for those minutes.

A break, not a permanent unlock

Here is the part worth knowing before you download. Walking does not unlock your apps for good. It buys time: a break of the length you choose, after which blocking returns by itself. Inside the app we say “blocking” and “break” for that reason. People search for “unlock”, so this page uses that word where it names the phrase.

Minutes you don’t use carry over into tomorrow, then drop off the card. A walk in the morning can pay for a break in the evening, and a quiet day does not wipe out yesterday’s minutes.

What you can set

  • Your pace, from 100 to 1,000 steps a minute. See steps per minute.
  • When blocking runs: all the time, or only the days and hours you pick. See blocking hours.
  • How many emergency breaks you get a day, from 0 to 3.

What it does not do

Screen Walk is not a parental control app, and it does not watch anyone else’s phone. It is a tool for the person who owns the phone, so you can turn blocking off yourself. That is deliberate and delayed: you press and hold in Settings, and it takes effect at a coming midnight at least 12 hours away. You can cancel it until then. It will not work without Apple Health access, because the steps come from there. And it is iPhone only, in English.

What it costs

Some apps in this group charge a subscription. Screen Walk is 7 free days, then one payment, with nothing that renews. Details are on the price page.

Questions

Is Screen Walk a parental control app?

No. It is a self-control tool for your own phone.

Does the break end on its own?

Yes. When the minutes you chose are up, blocking comes back by itself, even if Screen Walk is closed.

What if I have no minutes?

Today offers an emergency break when the card holds fewer than 5 minutes. It lasts 5 minutes and uses none.
