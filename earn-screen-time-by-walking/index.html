Earn screen time by walking

Walk, and Screen Walk tops up minutes on a card. By default 250 steps top up 1 minute, and you spend the minutes on timed breaks.

The short answer

Walk, and the minutes appear on your card. With Screen Walk the exchange is plain: 250 steps top up 1 minute unless you set another pace. Walk 2,500 steps and you have 10 minutes to spend on a break.

People call this earning screen time. Inside Screen Walk we say walking tops up minutes, because the card is something you fill, and a break is something you spend.

How the steps are counted

Steps come from Apple Health. That includes an iPhone in your pocket and an Apple Watch on your wrist. Steps typed into the Health app by hand are ignored, and Screen Walk never writes to Health. Minutes are counted in whole minutes, and the card shows how many steps are left until the next one.

Spending what you topped up

When you want your apps, start a break of 5 to 60 minutes and press and hold your card on the reader. The apps open for that long, and blocking returns on its own. Minutes you don’t use carry over into tomorrow, then drop off the card.

What it does not do

  • It does not count push-ups, workouts or anything but steps. Some other apps in this group do.
  • It is not a fitness tracker. Step and calorie goals are optional and never change your minutes.
  • It does not shame a short day. There are no streaks, and the card is never shown in red.

You choose the pace, so a walk can mean as much or as little as you want. See how to pick a pace.

Questions

How many steps do I need for a minute?

250 by default. You can change it from 100 to 1,000 steps in steps of 50.

Do steps from an Apple Watch count?

Yes, they come from Apple Health along with your iPhone’s.

Can I top up minutes with anything but walking?

No. Walking is the only way, and steps typed in by hand are ignored.
