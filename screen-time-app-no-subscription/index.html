A screen time app with no subscription

Screen Walk is 7 free days, then one payment. There is no subscription, nothing renews, and no account is needed.

The price in plain words

You start with 7 free days, and nothing is charged to start them. When the days are over, Screen Walk asks for one payment. That is the whole price: $9.99 in the United States, and in other countries the local price Apple shows in the app.

What stops after the free days

If you do not make the payment, topping up minutes and starting breaks wait until you do. The free days cannot be started a second time. If you ever need your purchase on a new phone, Settings has Restore purchases.

Why one payment

Some apps in this group charge a subscription, with weekly, monthly or yearly plans. Screen Walk has no plan to renew and nothing to forget about. You pay once and the app is yours.

Other apps use other arrangements. We do not claim to be the only app that avoids a subscription. We claim only what is true of Screen Walk: 7 free days, then one payment.

What you get for it

  • Walking that tops up minutes on a fare card, at a pace you choose.
  • Timed breaks of 5 to 60 minutes, and emergency breaks.
  • Blocking hours: all the time, or only the days and hours you pick.
  • Today, Insights and optional goals.

What about my data

There is no account. Your step counts, your Health data and the apps you choose stay on your phone. The app does send anonymous usage events to our own server, so we can see where people get stuck. It is not a “no tracking” app, and the privacy policy lists what is sent.

Earlier subscriptions

Monthly and yearly plans were sold before the one payment arrived. They are no longer for sale. If you started one, manage it in the App Store app under your profile › Subscriptions.

Questions

Is Screen Walk free?

You get 7 free days, with nothing charged to start them. After that it is one payment.

How much is the one payment?

$9.99 in the United States. Other countries see Apple’s local price in the app.

Does anything renew?

No. There is no subscription, so there is nothing to cancel.

Do I need an account?

No account, and no ads.
