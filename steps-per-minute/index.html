How many steps equal a minute of screen time?

In Screen Walk, 250 steps top up 1 minute by default. You can set the pace anywhere from 100 to 1,000 steps for a minute.

The default and the range

Screen Walk starts at 250 steps for 1 minute. You can set the pace from 100 to 1,000 steps for a minute, in steps of 50. At 100, a 5,000-step day tops up 50 minutes. At 1,000, it tops up 5.

Choosing a pace

There is no right pace. 250 is a middle setting: a half-hour walk of about 3,000 steps tops up around 12 minutes. A lower number is generous, and a higher one makes every minute cost more walking. Pick the one that feels fair to you. You can change it later.

Onboarding shows what each pace costs on an example card, and Settings has a Pace row for changing it after that.

When a change starts

A harder pace, with more steps for a minute, starts at once. An easier pace, with fewer steps, starts at the beginning of the day, two days after you confirm it. The pause is on purpose: it keeps a weak moment from lowering the price of a break. While a change waits, Settings shows the date and lets you keep the current pace instead.

Where the steps come from

Apple Health: your iPhone or an Apple Watch. Steps typed in by hand are ignored. Screen Walk reads steps only and never writes to Health.

What this is not

The pace is a way to set how much walking a minute costs. It is not a health target or advice about how much to walk. Goals in Screen Walk are optional, and they never change your minutes.
