Walk to top up your screen time.

Screen Walk keeps blocking the apps and websites you choose on your iPhone. Walking tops up minutes on a fare card, and you hold the card on the reader to start a timed break.

7 free days, then one payment. No subscription.

How it works in three steps

  1. Choose what pulls you in. Pick apps and websites with Apple’s own picker. Screen Walk keeps blocking them, and the list stays on your phone.
  2. Walk. Steps come from Apple Health, from your phone or an Apple Watch. By default 250 steps top up 1 minute on your card.
  3. Start a break. Choose 5 to 60 minutes and press and hold your card on the reader. Your apps open for that long, and blocking comes back by itself when the break ends.

The card and the reader

Your minutes live on a transit-style fare card. Walking tops it up; a break uses minutes from it. There is no Start button to tap by accident: you press and hold the card on the reader, and the break begins when the reader confirms.

When the card is low, an emergency break gives you 5 minutes without using any. You get one a day by default, and Settings lets you choose 0 to 3.

Minutes you don’t use carry over into tomorrow, then drop off the card.

Your pace

250 steps top up 1 minute unless you say otherwise. You can set the pace anywhere from 100 to 1,000 steps a minute, in steps of 50, to match how much you want to walk. Here is what a day of walking tops up at a few paces.

Blocking hours

New in 1.4.0. Blocking can run all the time, which is the default, or only on the days and hours you pick. Outside those hours your apps are open, no minutes are used and walking still tops up the card.

Questions

Is Screen Walk a parental control app?

No. It is for your own use. You choose the apps and websites, and you decide when blocking runs. It does not monitor anyone else’s phone.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in, no name and no email to give.

Where do the steps come from?

From Apple Health, so a phone in your pocket or an Apple Watch both work. Steps typed into the Health app by hand are ignored.

What happens to minutes I don’t use?

Minutes you don’t use carry over into tomorrow, then drop off the card.

Can I turn blocking off?

Yes, on purpose and with a delay. You press and hold in Settings, then blocking turns off at a coming midnight at least 12 hours away. Until then you can cancel it.

Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026

The short version: Screen Walk has no accounts, no ads and no cross-app tracking. Your steps and your app selections are processed on your iPhone and never leave it. To improve the app, it sends usage data that is not tied to your name or email to our own server (see Usage data below). The only third parties involved are Apple (for purchases), RevenueCat (to validate those purchases) and Cloudflare (which runs our server).

What the app reads on your device

  • Step count (Apple Health). With your permission, Screen Walk reads your daily step count from Apple Health to convert steps into unlock minutes. It never writes to Health, never reads other health data, and never sends step data anywhere. Steps typed into the Health app by hand are ignored.
  • Apps and websites you choose to lock (Screen Time). Screen Walk uses Apple's Family Controls, Managed Settings and Device Activity frameworks. Apple's picker returns opaque tokens for your selection; the app cannot see which apps they are, and the tokens stay on your device.
  • Your settings and minute balance (steps-per-minute rate, minutes spent, break timers) are stored locally on the device. Your steps-per-minute setting is also part of the usage data described below; your minute balance and break lengths are not.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple. To confirm that a purchase is active, the app sends the App Store receipt and an anonymous, randomly generated app user ID to RevenueCat, our subscription infrastructure provider. RevenueCat does not receive your name, email, health data or app selections. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Usage data

To see where people get stuck and improve the app, we collect usage data that is not tied to your name or email: which screens you open; which setup steps you reach and the options you pick there, including your steps-per-minute setting; whether Screen Time, Motion & Fitness and notification access was allowed, and that Health access was requested (never whether it was granted, and never anything Health holds); when you change your steps per minute or your goal settings; when a break or an emergency break starts, and when you change how many emergency breaks you get a day; whether blocking is switched on or off, and when you schedule or cancel turning it off; whether the paywall was shown, which plan you looked at and which reason (if any) you tapped; whether a purchase or restore succeeded and, if not, the error code; and whether your plan is active. When you change your blocking hours, Screen Walk records that you changed them, whether you chose all the time or set hours, and whether the change started at once, will start later, or was kept as it was. The days and times you choose never leave your phone. Each event carries the app version and a random ID that RevenueCat creates for your installation. It is sent to our own Cloudflare Worker and stored in our own database. It never includes your step count, Health data, how many minutes you have, the length of your breaks, your goals, the days and times of your blocking hours, or which apps and websites you chose; those stay on your phone. We use no third-party analytics SDK, run no advertising SDKs and do no cross-app tracking.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in. We do not collect your name, email or contacts.
  • No advertising, no third-party analytics SDKs, no tracking across apps or websites, and no sale of data.
  • Our own server receives only the usage data described above, never your name, email, step counts, Health data or app selections.

Notifications

The app schedules local notifications on your device (for example when a break ends). Nothing is sent to a push service.

Screen Walk is a self-control tool for the device owner and is not directed at children under 13. It is not a parental-control product and does not monitor anyone else's device.

Frequently asked

Why can’t I start a break? A break needs at least 5 minutes on your card. Minutes come from steps recorded in Apple Health, at your pace (250 steps for a minute unless you changed it), and minutes you topped up today and yesterday are both on the card. If the card holds fewer than 5 minutes, Today offers an emergency break instead. If you set blocking hours, a break can’t run past the end of the hours, so the longest choice may be shorter.

My steps do not show up. Make sure Screen Walk has read access under Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices, and that your iPhone or Apple Watch is recording steps. Steps entered by hand in the Health app do not count.

How do I turn blocking off? In Screen Walk, open Settings and press and hold Turn off blocking for 15 seconds, then confirm. A pop-up names the real date: blocking turns off at a coming midnight, at least 12 hours after you confirm, so the wait is 12 to 36 hours. Until then, Keep blocking on cancels it. Turning blocking back on is never delayed.

How do I change when blocking runs? Open Settings and choose Blocking hours. All the time is the default; Chosen hours lets you pick days, a start time and an end time. Adding hours starts at once. Choosing fewer hours waits like turning blocking off, and Keep current hours cancels the change until then.

What is an emergency break? A 5-minute break that uses no minutes, for when the card is low. You get one a day by default; Settings lets you choose 0 to 3. The count starts over each day.

What happens after the 7 free days? Screen Walk asks for the one payment. Topping up minutes and starting breaks wait until you have paid. Nothing renews, and no subscription is started.

How do I restore a purchase? Settings › Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple ID used for the purchase.

Is there a subscription to cancel? No subscription is sold now. If you started a monthly or yearly plan earlier, manage it in the App Store app: your profile › Subscriptions.
