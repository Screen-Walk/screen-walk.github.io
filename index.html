Screen Walk

Your apps stay locked until you walk.

Screen Walk turns your steps into screen time. Pick the apps and websites that pull you in; every 250 steps you take earns a minute of screen time. Spend it on a timed break, then the lock comes back on its own. Unspent minutes roll over one day, then expire.

Privacy Policy

Effective 12 October 2026

The short version: Screen Walk has no accounts, no ads and no cross-app tracking. Your steps and your app selections are processed on your iPhone and never leave it. To improve the app, it sends usage data that is not tied to your name or email to our own server (see Usage data below). The only third parties involved are Apple (for purchases), RevenueCat (to validate those purchases) and Cloudflare (which runs our server).

What the app reads on your device

  • Step count (Apple Health). With your permission, Screen Walk reads your daily step count from Apple Health to convert steps into unlock minutes. It never writes to Health, never reads other health data, and never sends step data anywhere. Steps typed into the Health app by hand are ignored.
  • Apps and websites you choose to lock (Screen Time). Screen Walk uses Apple's Family Controls, Managed Settings and Device Activity frameworks. Apple's picker returns opaque tokens for your selection; the app cannot see which apps they are, and the tokens stay on your device.
  • Your settings and minute balance (steps-per-minute rate, minutes spent, break timers) are stored locally on the device. Your steps-per-minute setting is also part of the usage data described below; your minute balance and break lengths are not.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple. To confirm that a purchase is active, the app sends the App Store receipt and an anonymous, randomly generated app user ID to RevenueCat, our subscription infrastructure provider. RevenueCat does not receive your name, email, health data or app selections. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Usage data

To see where people get stuck and improve the app, we collect usage data that is not tied to your name or email: which screens you open; which setup steps you reach and the options you pick there, including your steps-per-minute setting; whether Screen Time, Motion & Fitness and notification access was allowed, and that Health access was requested (never whether it was granted, and never anything Health holds); when you change your steps per minute or your goal settings; when a break or an emergency break starts, and when you change how many emergency breaks you get a day; whether blocking is switched on or off, and when you schedule or cancel turning it off; whether the paywall was shown, which plan you looked at and which reason (if any) you tapped; whether a purchase or restore succeeded and, if not, the error code; and whether your plan is active. Each event carries the app version and a random ID that RevenueCat creates for your installation. It is sent to our own Cloudflare Worker and stored in our own database. It never includes your step count, Health data, how many minutes you have, the length of your breaks, your goals, or which apps and websites you chose; those stay on your phone. We use no third-party analytics SDK, run no advertising SDKs and do no cross-app tracking.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in. We do not collect your name, email or contacts.
  • No advertising, no third-party analytics SDKs, no tracking across apps or websites, and no sale of data.
  • Our own server receives only the usage data described above, never your name, email, step counts, Health data or app selections.

Notifications

The app schedules local notifications on your device (for example when a break ends). Nothing is sent to a push service.

Children

Screen Walk is a self-control tool for the device owner and is not directed at children under 13. It is not a parental-control product and does not monitor anyone else's device.

Your choices

You can revoke Health access in Settings › Health › Data Access, revoke Screen Time access in Settings › Screen Time, and delete all the app's data on your device by deleting the app. Subscriptions are managed in your App Store account settings.

Frequently asked

Why is "Spend minutes" disabled? You need at least 5 minutes in your balance. Minutes come from steps recorded in Apple Health today or yesterday, at your chosen rate (default 250 steps per minute).

My steps do not show up. Make sure Screen Walk has read access under Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices, and that your iPhone or Apple Watch is recording steps. Steps entered by hand in the Health app do not count.

How do I turn blocking off? Settings › press and hold "Turn off blocking" for 15 seconds. Your apps unlock immediately and stay unlocked until you turn it back on from Home.

How do I restore a purchase? Settings › Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple ID used for the purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription? In the App Store app: your profile › Subscriptions, or Settings › Manage subscription inside Screen Walk.
