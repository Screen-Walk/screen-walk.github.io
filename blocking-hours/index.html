Block apps on a schedule with blocking hours

Screen Walk can block your chosen apps all the time, which is the default, or only on the days and hours you pick.

All the time, or chosen hours

Blocking hours are new in Screen Walk 1.4.0. The setting has two choices. All the time is the default and works as before: blocking keeps running until you start a break. Chosen hours lets you pick the days of the week and one start time and one end time, the same on each chosen day.

What happens outside the hours

Outside your hours your apps are open. No minutes are used there, and walking still tops up the card, so the minutes are waiting when the hours begin. Screen Walk sends no notification when the hours start or end.

What you can choose

  • Any days of the week, at least one.
  • A start time and an end time, in steps of 5 minutes.
  • Overnight hours, for example ending the next morning.
  • A length from 15 minutes up to 23 hours. For a full day, choose All the time.

Breaks inside the hours

A break never runs past the end of your hours, so the longest choice on the Start a break sheet is shorter near the end. An emergency break is available only inside the hours, since outside them your apps are open anyway.

Changing your hours

Adding hours starts at once. Choosing fewer hours waits, like turning blocking off: the change starts at a coming midnight at least 12 hours away, a pop-up names the real date, and Keep current hours cancels it until then. The wait protects the plan you made when you were calm from the mood you are in later.

What it does not do

  • It does not offer different hours for different days.
  • It is not a parental control. You set your own hours on your own phone.
  • It needs a real iPhone: Apple’s Screen Time technology does not run in a simulator.

You can also set hours during setup: the Screen Time step has a row already set to All the time.

Questions

What is the default?

All the time. Blocking runs non-stop, as it always has, until you choose hours.

Can I set different hours for different days?

No. There is one set: your chosen days, one start time and one end time.

How do the hours start and stop?

Screen Walk sets your hours with iOS, using Apple’s Device Activity technology, and blocking follows them.

Does Screen Walk send a notification at the start or end?

No.
